# 🚦 Priority Levels



Priority determines how quickly a ticket must be responded to and resolved. It is based on impact and urgency.



## Priority Reference

| Priority | Meaning | Response Time | Example |
|----------|---------|---------------|--------|
| 🔴 Critical | Business completely stopped. Multiple users affected. | 15 minutes | Server down. Full network outage. All staff cannot work. |
| 🟠 High | One user or team cannot do their job. | 1 hour | Manager cannot access email. VPN not working for remote worker. |
| 🟡 Medium | User affected but can partially work. Workaround exists. | 4 hours | Outlook sync slow but works. Printer down — user can email. |
| 🟢 Low | Minor issue. No business impact. Can wait. | 24 hours | Desktop wallpaper change request. Mouse double-clicking occasionally. |

---

## How Priority is Decided

> **Impact** - how many people are affected and how severely.

> **Urgency** - how quickly it needs to be fixed.

> Impact + Urgency = Priority
