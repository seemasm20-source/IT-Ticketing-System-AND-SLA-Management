# 📜 What is a Service Level Agreement (SLA)?


An **SLA (Service Level Agreement)** is a formal agreement between an IT service provider and the business that defines the **expected response times**, **resolution times** and **service standards** for each priority level of ticket.

---

## Why SLAs Matter

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| Sets expectations | Users know how long they will wait for a fix |
| Measures performance | IT team is held accountable to agreed timeframes |
| Prioritises work | Critical tickets must be handled before low ones |
| Protects business | Ensures critical systems are restored quickly |
